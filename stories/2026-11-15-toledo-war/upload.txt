TITLE:
Ohio and Michigan went to war over Toledo

DESCRIPTION:
In 1835, Ohio and the Michigan Territory both claimed the Toledo Strip, 468 square miles along their border, because the boundary set in 1787 relied on maps that put the southern tip of Lake Michigan too far north. Both sides called out their militias, but no one was killed. Congress then made giving up the strip the price of statehood, and in exchange Michigan received about 9,000 square miles of the western Upper Peninsula, where valuable copper and iron ore were later found. Michigan became a state on January 26, 1837.
