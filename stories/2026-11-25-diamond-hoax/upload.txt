TITLE:
Two cousins fooled Tiffany by planting diamonds

DESCRIPTION:
Two Kentucky cousins scattered gems bought in London on a remote mesa near the Wyoming-Colorado border and sold the "diamond field" to San Francisco bankers. Geologist Clarence King exposed the hoax in November 1872.
