TITLE:
L.A. fired 1,440 shells at planes that weren't there

DESCRIPTION:
On February 25, 1942, days after a Japanese submarine shelled an oil field near Santa Barbara, anti-aircraft guns in Los Angeles fired 1,440 shells at an enemy that never came.
