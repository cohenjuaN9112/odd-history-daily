TITLE:
At 63, she rode a barrel over Niagara Falls

DESCRIPTION:
On October 24, 1901, her 63rd birthday, schoolteacher Annie Edson Taylor became the first person to survive a trip over Niagara Falls in a barrel. The widow hoped the stunt would pay for her old age: days after a cat survived a test run, she went over the 167-foot Horseshoe Falls in an oak-and-iron barrel padded with a mattress, with air pumped in by a bicycle pump. She came out with only a small gash on her head, but her manager, Frank M. Russell, ran off with the barrel, and she died penniless in 1921.
