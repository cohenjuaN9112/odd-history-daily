TITLE:
'Mary Had a Little Lamb' author made Thanksgiving national

DESCRIPTION:
Sarah Josepha Hale, author of "Mary Had a Little Lamb," lobbied for years for one national Thanksgiving. In 1863, five days after her letter, Lincoln proclaimed the last Thursday of November.
