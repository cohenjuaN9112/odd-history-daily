TITLE:
A volcano in Indonesia helped create Frankenstein

DESCRIPTION:
In April 1815, Mount Tambora on the island of Sumbawa, in today's Indonesia, exploded in the largest volcanic eruption in recorded history. Its ash and sulfur haze cooled the climate, and 1816 became known as the year without a summer: snow fell in New England in June. About 7,700 miles from the volcano, at Lake Geneva, a cold, wet summer kept Lord Byron, Percy Shelley, 18-year-old Mary Shelley (then Mary Godwin) and John Polidori indoors, and Byron challenged them to write ghost stories. Mary began Frankenstein, published in 1818, and Polidori wrote The Vampyre, published in 1819.
