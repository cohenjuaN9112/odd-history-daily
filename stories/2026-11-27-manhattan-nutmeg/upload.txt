TITLE:
The Dutch traded Manhattan for a nutmeg island

DESCRIPTION:
In the 1667 Treaty of Breda, the Dutch kept Run, a tiny nutmeg island in Indonesia, while England kept Manhattan, which it had seized and renamed New York.
