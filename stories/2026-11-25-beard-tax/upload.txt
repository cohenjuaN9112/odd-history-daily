TITLE:
Russia once made men pay to keep their beards

DESCRIPTION:
In 1698, Peter the Great taxed beards to make Russia look more European. Men who paid carried a copper token stamped "money taken." Catherine the Great repealed the tax in 1772.

Sources: Smithsonian, British Museum, Atlas Obscura
