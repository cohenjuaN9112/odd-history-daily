TITLE:
In 1859, telegraphs ran on the northern lights

DESCRIPTION:
In September 1859, a huge solar storm lit up skies as far south as Cuba. Telegraphs sparked and failed, and operators in Boston kept sending messages to Maine with their batteries unplugged.
