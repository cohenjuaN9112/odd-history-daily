TITLE:
In 1939, America had two Thanksgivings

DESCRIPTION:
In 1939, President Roosevelt moved Thanksgiving a week earlier to give stores more shopping days before Christmas. Many states refused, and in 1941 Congress fixed it on the fourth Thursday.
